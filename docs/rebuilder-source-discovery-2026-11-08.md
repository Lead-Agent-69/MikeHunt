## First collector verification

- D & A and Philips are wired into the curated dealer runner. A read-only run against the real sites returned five photo-bearing vehicles per dealer. D & A disclosed four salvage titles and one clean title with total-loss history. Philips did not explicitly disclose a title brand on the five admitted as-is records, so their title stays unknown.
- The sampled D & A Equinox photo and Philips F-150 photo both returned HTTP 200 with JPEG content. The Philips F-150's as-is amount was $18,800 on both pages. The $24,995 base amount was excluded.
- Car Point Utah's footer identifies its displayed listings as samples. Keep it pending until an actual stock feed can be verified. HRM's linked inventory could not be independently checked in this pass.
- Additional leads for previously open states include [208 Auto Sales in Idaho](https://208auto.com/), [Parkway Auto Center in Washington](https://parkwayautocenter.gotgoodcars.com/certified-used-inventory/page/26/) and [In and Out Auto Sales in Kansas](https://in-and-out-auto-sales.landed.page/). Check licensing, actual vehicle photographs, stable identifiers, current stock and collection access before promotion.
