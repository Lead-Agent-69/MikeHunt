## Submission template

Subject: Authorized vehicle-inventory integration request for MikeHunt

Hello,

MikeHunt helps buyers find and compare vehicle listings with source attribution and links back to the
original seller. We would like an authorized integration for your participating inventory.

Please confirm whether you offer an API, feed, partner export or written permission for specified
public inventory routes. We need clarification on:

- Covered dealers, exact hosts and API/feed/website routes; whether dealer consent is also required.
- Fields available, stable listing identifiers, updates/removals and sold-price meaning.
- Permission to display listing fields/photos and produce derived comparable-price or ranking analytics.
- Required attribution/link-outs, cache/retention limits and correction/takedown procedures.
- Request quotas, permitted cadence, authentication, fees, review/expiry and revocation process.

We will not bypass access restrictions or publish inventory until the permitted scope is reviewed.
Please direct us to your integration contact and current agreement/application process.

Thank you,
Jonah / MikeHunt

## Approval handling

Record source ID, exact host, API/feed/website route, public evidence reference, review/expiry timestamps
and separate collection/display/derive rights using `AccessGrant`. Keep private evidence in the secure
operator system, not the repository. Verify the contract's scope and attribution before adding the
grant; conduct a small re-observation canary. Never translate a key signup or acknowledgement email
into unrestricted collection/republication permission.

Reference: [eBay developer portal](https://developer.ebay.com/),
[api.data.gov signup](https://api.data.gov/signup/). Confirm program availability at submission time.
